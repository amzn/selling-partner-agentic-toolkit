description: Diagnose why your Amazon sales dropped and get safe next steps

Use the **seller-analytics** and **listing-issues** skills against my Selling Partner account.

Establish the time window and a prior-period baseline, pull orders for both, then rank the usual causes (suppressed/inactive listings, stock-outs, buy-box/pricing, account health, ad-spend changes) using the Selling Partner Connector (MCP) tools. Report the revenue delta first, attribute it to specific ASINs/causes, and propose next steps — mark each as read-only insight vs requires-approval action. If pricing or Buy Box comes up as a cause, report it as diagnosis only: do not recommend raising or lowering any price. Do not apply any write.
